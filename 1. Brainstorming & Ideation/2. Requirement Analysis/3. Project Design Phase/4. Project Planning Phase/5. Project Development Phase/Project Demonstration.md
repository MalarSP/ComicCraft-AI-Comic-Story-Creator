# Project Demonstration

## Project Title
ComicCraft – AI Comic Story Creator

## Demonstration Overview

The demonstration presents the complete working process of the ComicCraft application, from entering a comic topic to generating the AI content and downloading the comic as a PDF.

## Demonstration Flow

1. Open the ComicCraft application.
2. Enter a comic topic in the input field.
3. Click the Generate Comic button.
4. Google Gemini generates the comic story.
5. Hugging Face FLUX generates the comic-style image.
6. The generated story and image are displayed on the webpage.
7. Click the Download Your Comic as PDF button.
8. The generated PDF is downloaded.
9. The download success page is displayed.

## Project Benefits

- Easy AI-based comic creation.
- Simple and user-friendly interface.
- Automatic story generation.
- AI-generated comic illustration.
- PDF export functionality.
